So the question that needs answered here is: Why this site? And while there are countless florid answers to explain why a new website needs to exist, 
the simple answer to why Easy Bonsai needs to exist is the very obvious: Entry level sites on this hobby are hard to read, hard to look at, and very 
difficult to call "entry level". It's a hobby with a lot of elitism, it makes a lot of assumptions about the hobbyist's knowledge. The only 'user story' 
that is important in this case is the one of the absolute, new beginner, finding it difficult to get a foothold in a hobby that takes multiple years to 
even truly begin. 

As a result, a stylish, single-page website, with the most basic of information. It utilizes a lively colour scheme and eye-catching images that direct 
the user's eyes to more information, sending them effortlessly to the pages that they require. This is accomplished by the inclusion of a simple navbar.
In future iterations, interactive cards will also be included creating a smoother user experience.

While I find the requirement of site-owner requirements being met (ie marketing or sales) appalling, I understand the necessity of it being included and
have thus added a required field on the contact form that opts users into marketing emails.

See included wireframe.
